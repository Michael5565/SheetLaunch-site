Open a presentation in four steps

SheetLaunch helps when you receive a PPTX or PPT presentation but collaborate in Google Slides. The original presentation stays on your computer while a Google copy is created in Drive.

  1. Connect your Google account in SheetLaunch.
  2. Find the PPTX or PPT presentation.
  3. Use Windows Open with and choose SheetLaunch.
  4. Check the slides in Google Slides before presenting or sharing.

Review these slide elements

Most ordinary text, images, shapes, and simple layouts are easy to continue editing. Check fonts, animations, transitions, speaker notes, embedded video, charts, audio, SmartArt, and unusual slide masters because imported presentations may look different.

Reuse existing presentations

Enable file reuse if you want the same local presentation to return to its existing Drive copy. This is helpful when you repeatedly open a deck during a project.

If the presentation will not open

Confirm that the file has finished downloading and that it uses PPTX or PPT. Reconnect Google sign-in if necessary, and try a smaller test presentation to identify conversion problems.
