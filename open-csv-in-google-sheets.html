Open CSV data without manual upload

CSV files are widely used for exports, reports, contact lists, and data transfers. SheetLaunch sends the selected CSV to your Google Drive and opens it in Google Sheets so you can sort, filter, format, and share it.

  1. Sign in with the Google account you want to use.
  2. Locate the CSV file in Windows Explorer.
  3. Choose Open with, then SheetLaunch.
  4. Check the imported columns and values in Google Sheets.

Check separators and encoding

CSV files can use commas, semicolons, or tabs as separators, and they may use different character encodings. If columns appear merged or accented characters look wrong, import the data again with the appropriate separator and encoding settings in Google Sheets.

Keep data safe

The original CSV remains unchanged. Review Drive sharing permissions before sending the converted spreadsheet to other people, especially if it contains contact details or business data.

Reuse or create a fresh copy

File reuse can reopen an existing Google Sheets copy. Disable reuse when you intentionally need a new import from an updated or differently formatted CSV.
