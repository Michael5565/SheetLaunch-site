Supported file conversions

  • Excel Spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls): Converted automatically into editable Google Sheets documents.
  • Word Documents (.docx, .doc): Converted automatically into editable Google Docs documents.
  • PowerPoint Presentations (.pptx, .ppt): Converted automatically into editable Google Slides presentations.
  • CSV Data Files (.csv): Imported cleanly into Google Sheets for sorting and editing.

How automated conversion works

Select any supported document through Windows Open with, choose SheetLaunch, and the application securely uploads the selected file to your connected Google Drive account. The corresponding Google Workspace editor opens immediately in your web browser.

What happens to the original local file?

Your original file remains completely untouched on your computer. The newly converted Google Workspace document is stored safely in your connected Google Drive, giving you full control over sharing and permissions.
