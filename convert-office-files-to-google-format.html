OFFICE FILE CONVERTER

Convert Office files to Google formats.

Move from Microsoft file formats to editable Google Workspace copies without manually navigating Google Drive each time.

Supported conversions

Excel files such as XLSX and XLS open in Google Sheets. Word documents such as DOCX and DOC open in Google Docs. PowerPoint presentations such as PPTX and PPT open in Google Slides. CSV files open in Google Sheets.

How conversion works

Choose a file through Windows Open with, select SheetLaunch, and the app uploads the selected file to your connected Google Drive. The matching Workspace editor then opens in your browser.

What happens to the original?

Your original local file remains unchanged. The Google Workspace version is created in your Drive, where you control its storage and sharing settings.
