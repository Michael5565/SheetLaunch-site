How it works

Install SheetLaunch, sign in with Google, then choose SheetLaunch from the file’s Windows Open with menu. Your document opens in an editable Google Docs copy.

Is my document stored by SheetLaunch?

The document is sent to your connected Google Drive. SheetLaunch does not retain document contents on its own servers.
