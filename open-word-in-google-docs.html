How it works

Install SheetLaunch, sign in with Google, then choose SheetLaunch from the file’s Windows Open with menu. Your document opens in an editable Google Docs copy.

Is my document stored by SheetLaunch?

The document is sent to your connected Google Drive. SheetLaunch does not retain document contents on its own servers.

Opening Word files step by step

DOCX files are common in applications, school work, contracts, and shared documents. SheetLaunch lets you move from the local file to an editable Google Docs copy without manually opening Drive and uploading first.

  1. Sign in to SheetLaunch with Google.
  2. Find the DOCX or DOC file on your computer.
  3. Right-click it and choose SheetLaunch from Open with.
  4. Review the converted document in Google Docs before sharing it.

Formatting and compatibility

Headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, images, and common styles usually transfer well. Review page breaks, headers, footers, fonts, tracked changes, comments, embedded objects, and precise layouts. Keep the original Word document until you approve the result.

Sharing the converted document

Google Docs controls sharing through Drive. Choose whether collaborators can view, comment, or edit. SheetLaunch does not automatically make the document public.

Troubleshooting

Use a completed DOCX download rather than a temporary browser file. If the app is missing from Open with, choose another app and browse to the installed SheetLaunch application. Conversion requires an internet connection.
