DOCX HELP

How to open DOCX files without Word.

Open DOCX documents in Google Docs from Windows without launching Microsoft Word or manually uploading through Drive.

Download SheetLaunch Learn more

Step-by-step guide

  1. Install SheetLaunch and connect your Google account.
  2. Right-click the DOCX file in Windows Explorer.
  3. Choose Open with, then SheetLaunch.
  4. Continue editing in Google Docs in your web browser.

Will document formatting be preserved?

Google Docs supports standard DOCX formatting, including paragraphs, headings, bullet lists, tables, and images. Complex typography, unusual fonts, or multi-column page layouts may need minor adjustments after conversion. Your original file stays safely on your computer.
