PRIVACY

Your files stay yours.

SheetLaunch sends selected documents directly to your connected Google Drive account for conversion. SheetLaunch does not sell, inspect, or retain document contents. When file reuse is enabled, the app stores a local fingerprint, file ID, and Workspace URL so it can reopen an existing copy.

Google account tokens are stored in the operating system’s secure credential storage. We collect only the information needed to provide the service, support accounts, enforce access, and prevent abuse.

TERMS
