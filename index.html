FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What files does SheetLaunch open?
SheetLaunch opens Excel (.xlsx, .xls), Word (.docx, .doc), PowerPoint (.pptx, .ppt) and CSV files directly in their corresponding Google Workspace apps (Google Sheets, Docs, and Slides).
Does SheetLaunch store my documents?
No. Selected files are sent directly and securely to your connected Google Drive account. SheetLaunch does not inspect, sell, or retain document contents on server infrastructure.
Can SheetLaunch reopen an existing Google copy?
Yes! Enabling file reuse allows SheetLaunch to reopen an existing Google Drive copy when you launch the same file again, avoiding clutter and duplicates.
PRIVACY & TERMS

Your files stay yours.

SheetLaunch sends selected documents directly to your connected Google Drive account for conversion. SheetLaunch does not sell, inspect, or retain document contents.
