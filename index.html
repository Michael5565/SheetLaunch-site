PRIVACY & TERMS

Your files stay yours.

SheetLaunch sends selected documents directly to your connected Google Drive account for conversion. SheetLaunch does not sell, inspect, or retain document contents.

Google account tokens are stored in the operating system’s secure credential storage. We collect only the information needed to provide the service, support accounts, enforce access, and prevent abuse.

Simple, fair terms.

Use SheetLaunch only with files you have permission to upload and convert. You are responsible for your Google account, Drive storage, and compliance with Google Workspace rules.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What files does SheetLaunch open?

SheetLaunch opens Excel, Word, PowerPoint and CSV files in the matching Google Sheets, Docs or Slides editor.

Does SheetLaunch store my documents?

The selected document is sent to your connected Google Drive. SheetLaunch does not retain document contents on its own servers.

Can SheetLaunch reopen an existing Google copy?

Yes. Enable file reuse to reopen an existing Google Drive copy instead of creating a duplicate.
