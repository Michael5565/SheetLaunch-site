Why traditional document workflows are tedious

Opening an email attachment usually involves downloading it, finding Google Drive in your web browser, uploading the file, waiting for conversion, and locating the newly created document. Repeating these steps multiple times a day creates unnecessary friction.

A faster desktop routine

With SheetLaunch installed, simply use Open with on any local Office or CSV file. The application seamlessly manages the Google Drive handoff and opens the matching Google editor in seconds. You can also configure SheetLaunch to reuse existing Drive copies for repeat opens.

Who benefits from SheetLaunch?

SheetLaunch is ideal for students, contractors, agency teams, remote workers, and enterprise professionals who collaborate in Google Workspace while receiving files in legacy Microsoft formats.
