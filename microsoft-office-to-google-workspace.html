From Microsoft Office to Google Workspace.

SheetLaunch is for people who receive Office files but do their everyday editing in Google Docs, Sheets or Slides.

Why the workflow is frustrating

Opening an attachment often means downloading it, finding Google Drive, uploading it, waiting for conversion, and locating the new file. Repeating those steps interrupts your work.

A faster routine

With SheetLaunch installed, use Open with on the original Office or CSV file. The app handles the Drive handoff and opens the correct Google editor. You can choose whether repeated opens reuse an existing copy.

Who is it for?

It is useful for students, remote teams, contractors, and anyone collaborating in Google Workspace while still receiving Microsoft file formats.
