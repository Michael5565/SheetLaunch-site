GOOGLE DRIVE WORKFLOW

Open Office files in Google Drive.

There are several ways to open Microsoft Office files in Google Workspace. SheetLaunch removes the repeated upload-and-open steps.

The manual method

Open Google Drive, choose New, select File upload, wait for the upload, then open the file and choose the appropriate Google editor. This works, but it is slow when you receive files regularly.

The SheetLaunch method

Install SheetLaunch, sign in once, then use Windows Open with on an Excel, Word, PowerPoint or CSV file. SheetLaunch sends it to your Google Drive and opens the matching editor in your browser.

Can it reuse an existing copy?

Yes. Turn on file reuse in the app to reopen the existing Google Drive copy for a file instead of creating another one.
