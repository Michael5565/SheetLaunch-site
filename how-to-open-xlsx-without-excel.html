How to open XLSX files without Excel.

Use Google Sheets as an alternative editor for XLSX workbooks and open them directly from your computer with SheetLaunch.

Steps

  1. Install SheetLaunch and sign in with Google.
  2. Right-click the XLSX file in Windows Explorer.
  3. Choose Open with, then SheetLaunch.
  4. Edit the converted copy in Google Sheets.

Important note

Complex Excel features, macros, fonts, and formulas may not convert perfectly. Review important workbooks after conversion. Your original XLSX file is not modified.
