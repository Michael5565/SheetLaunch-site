How to open XLSX files without Excel.

Use Google Sheets as a free alternative editor for XLSX workbooks and open them directly from your computer with SheetLaunch.

Download SheetLaunch Learn more

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install SheetLaunch and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Right-click the XLSX spreadsheet file in Windows Explorer.
  3. Choose Open with, then select SheetLaunch.
  4. Edit the converted copy directly in Google Sheets.

Important considerations

While standard formulas, tables, and formatting convert seamlessly, complex macros (VBA), custom fonts, external cell links, and specialized pivot tables may require review after import. Your original local XLSX file is never modified or overwritten.
